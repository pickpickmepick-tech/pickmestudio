Workflow

How a track gets made

음원 한 곡이 유튜브에 올라가기까지

  1. 01 Bars 1–4 Claude

    Plan and write

    Pick a genre and a channel concept. Claude writes the lyrics, the song structure and the Suno style prompt. Our prompt sets cover more than 30 genres, with 12 to 15 tracks each.

    장르와 채널 콘셉트를 정하면 Claude가 가사, 곡 구조, Suno 프롬프트를 씁니다.

  2. 02 Bars 4–7 Suno

    Generate the tracks

    Students run Claude's prompts in Suno and learn to pull 15 or more usable tracks from a single genre prompt.

    한 장르 프롬프트로 15곡 이상 뽑아내는 법을 배웁니다.

  3. 03 Bars 7–11 BandLab · Suno StudioClaude

    Edit, mix and master

    Tracks are finished in a DAW: BandLab for the basics, Suno Studio for advanced work. Claude reviews the arrangement and keeps the mix checklist, and the student makes the final calls by ear.

    최종 판단은 사람의 귀로. 휴먼터치 편집과 마스터링까지 다룹니다.

  4. 04 Bars 10–14 Higgsfield via MCP

    Make the music video

    Claude reads the lyrics, plans the scenes and drives Higgsfield through MCP to produce a lyric-based music video for the track.

    Claude가 가사를 읽고 장면을 짠 뒤, MCP로 힉스필드를 움직여 뮤직비디오를 만듭니다.

  5. 05 Bars 14–16 ClaudeYouTube

    Title it and upload

    Claude writes the playlist title, the description and the channel copy from the tracklist. Then the playlist goes up on YouTube.

    제목과 설명란은 Claude가 쓰고, 플레이리스트를 유튜브에 올립니다.
